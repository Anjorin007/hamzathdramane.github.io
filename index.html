Complete fintech solution enabling unbanked Beninese to obtain a credit score based on their Mobile Money transactions (MTN MoMo, Moov Money, Celtiis). The system includes an invisible Android application (Ghost Agent) that automatically captures transactional SMS, an AI backend that analyzes financial behaviors, and generates professional creditworthiness reports for microfinance institutions.
